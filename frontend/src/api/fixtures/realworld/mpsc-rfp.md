## 2. BACKGROUND

The Michigan Public Service Commission (MPSC) utilizes Salesforce.com for three Salesforce environments:

CAD/Telecom Salesforce Database, The Customer Assistance Division (CAD)/Telecom Database of the MPSC handles the data and all associated reporting of the 3 areas listed below. Overall, there are internal users (40 total) using MiLogin; external web portal users (1000 total); and associated web forms/pages.

E-Dockets, Case management system for MPSC's regulatory docket, public legal case data, and authorized Commission decisions related to regulated state energy and telecommunications utilities per Public Act 16 of 1929, MCL 483.1. System includes a database and all associated reporting for internal users (58 total); MiLogin access; web portal users (200 total); and associated web forms/pages.

Pipeline Safety, The Gas Safety and Operations Division of the MPSC maintains responsibility for monitoring the distribution, production, transmission, and underground storage of natural gas in Michigan. MPSC staff are a liaison with the federal pipeline safety officials to enforce gas safety standards and performs gas safety inspections. System includes a database and all associated reporting for internal users (34 total); MiLogin access; web portal users (30 total with the potential for approximately 100 users in the next year); and associated web forms/pages.

## 3. PURPOSE/CHALLENGE STATEMENT

Vendor will provide up to 600 hours of Salesforce maintenance and Support Services each year (including related applications like Conga) over the period of this SOW. These 600 hours will be divided equally to support the CAD/Telecom (200 hours), E-Dockets (200 hours) and Pipeline Safety (200 hours) Salesforce environments, as described in the background.
