## 2. BACKGROUND

The Youth Employment Standards Act (Public Act 90 of 1978) requires the Wage and Hour Division (WHD) to establish a statewide, no-cost work permit system for youth employment. This responsibility includes transitioning the permit processing duties from local school districts to the Department by replacing the current paper-based method with a centralized electronic system. WHD must ensure that the new system is accessible to minors, employers, and guardians and supports compliance with youth employment laws across Michigan. This transition is essential to improve efficiency, standardize enforcement, and uphold legal protections for working minors.
