## 2. BACKGROUND

The State of Michigan is seeking a commercial off-the-shelf laboratory information management system (LIMS) solution with modern and enhanced interoperability for its clinical diagnostic, genomics, environmental hazard, water, food, animal, and surveillance testing. The LIMS must meet the Public Health Laboratory’s business, functional, and technical needs for the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) Bureau of Laboratories (BOL) in Lansing, Michigan, and its Regional Laboratory partner sites throughout the state which can be found in **Schedule J – State of Michigan and Regional Laboratory Locations** .

The MDHHS BOL was founded in 1907. It is comprised of two divisions, Infectious Disease and Chemistry & Toxicology, along with the sections of Quality Assurance, Laboratory Systems and

Health & Safety. The BOL provides laboratory support for local health departments, other state health departments, hospitals, and physicians throughout the State of Michigan.

Fiscal Year 2024 Testing Summary statistics, from both MDHHS BOL Divisions for 150 tests include ~182,000 samples tested with ~550,000 analytes released (reported) from the LIMS _._

The Infectious Disease Division is designated as a Center for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) Laboratory Response Network Biological (LRN-B) Advanced Tier laboratory that tests biological samples for the CDC, the Federal Select Agent program, and the Food Emergency Response Network (FERN). It also provides quality reference and specialized testing services for rapid and effective detection and surveillance of emerging and existing communicable diseases. The Microbiology Section performs testing for a variety of threats such as foodborne illness, sexually transmitted infections, fungal disease as well as respiratory diseases like tuberculosis and pertussis. The Virology Section handles viral public health threats such as SARS CoV-19, Jamestown Canyon virus, rabies, and Eastern Equine Encephalitis virus. The Genomics Section assists epidemiologic efforts to trace and monitor emerging infectious diseases and their variants via genetic sequencing and data analysis.

The Chemistry & Toxicology Division is designated as a CDC Laboratory Response NetworkChemical (LRN-C) Level 1 laboratory that tests clinical samples in the event of a regional or national chemical exposure. The Clinical Chemistry Section of this division provides expertise in testing clinical specimens for human exposures from environmental contamination and the Environmental Chemistry Section provides testing for environmental samples for chemical contamination from agents such as, per and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs), and heavy metals.

Besides the State Public Health Laboratory (MDHHS BOL), there are six Regional Laboratory sites utilizing the State’s LIMS solution to provide laboratory test results for the residents of Michigan. They include Kalamazoo County Health & Community Services (HCS) Laboratory, Luce-MackinacAlger-Schoolcraft (LMAS) District Health Department Laboratory, Northern Michigan Regional Laboratory, Oakland County Health Division Laboratory – Pontiac, Oakland County Health Division Laboratory – Southfield, and Saginaw County Health Department Laboratory.

The LIMS is essential to the required functions of laboratory specimen test processing and test reporting for the MDHHS BOL and the Regional Laboratories. This includes but is not limited to users/roles/access controls, LIMS administration, sample lifecycle, sample tracking, quality control management, instrument interfacing, interoperability, ad hoc reports, billing, communication log, and Information Technology security.

The LIMS provides real time reporting of laboratory reports to hospitals, as well as local, state and federal health officials to prevent illness, disease, and deaths.
