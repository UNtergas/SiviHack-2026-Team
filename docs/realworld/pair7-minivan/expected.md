# Pair 7 — Michigan RFP 260000000513, Accessible Passenger Vehicle, Modified Minivan (MDOT)

Source: the State's Award Letter and Evaluation Synopsis. Two bids, both passed.

| Bidder | Technical score | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Hoekstra Transportation, Inc. | 100 / 100 | Award (BraunAbility and FR Conversions conversions, USD 3.89M–3.96M with options) |

The evaluators' only deficiencies, both on Bus Service: pricing for additional training was put
in the technical response (requirement 2.3), which the proposal instructions forbid; no
organisational chart for requirement 3.4.

## Files

Only Bus Service submitted the State template as a narrative response (`bsi.md`). Hoekstra's public
archive holds brochures, chassis specifications, crash-test and warranty documents and a dealer
letter, so it cannot be split into template answers and is not included.

## What a correct review should show

A hardware procurement with almost no narrative: a high coverage of the few asks, the training
pricing noticed as out of place, and few findings. The reviewer's rubric (problem understanding,
tone, risk) is tuned to services proposals; expect low scores that do not reflect the State's 96.
